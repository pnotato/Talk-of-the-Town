# Game Loop

Each round, a (weighted) random number of blobs start talking to another blob. After that, each blob reports its own perception of and interest in the company. The averages are collected and plotted for that round.

Blobs can also skip talking and post on social media instead. A blob who does neither may scroll the feed and read a post. Some blobs just idle because they don't care.

A game lasts 50 rounds. Every 10 rounds, you put out a news story that changes the blobs' perception.

Blob details are in [Blobs](./BLOBS.md).
